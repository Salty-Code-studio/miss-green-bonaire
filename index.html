Questions

Where do private trips leave from?

Our berth in the marina near Plaza Resort. Your booking confirmation comes with directions and a short video.

Can I pay without a credit card?

Yes. Bank transfer to our MCB Bonaire account, or cash in one payment.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen. Bring your own prescription mask if you need one. Shoes come off before boarding.

Can we bring our own float?

Sure. Tie it to your wrist with a rope. Bonaire is windy and floats drift.

What if we cancel?

Within 30 days 25%, within 3 days 50%, within 1 day the full price. No refund for no-shows.
