Home/The boat

Meet Miss Green, the quiet electric boat.

Bonaire's first fully electric catamaran.

Miss Green has two hulls and runs on electricity. She glides out to the reef without a roar, without fuel and without smoke.

Why electric?

Because the reef is our home too.

It is quiet under water
There is no engine noise to scare the fish.
No fuel or oil in the sea
Nothing leaks onto the coral. The water stays clean.
No fumes
You breathe fresh sea air, on the deck and in the water.

Life on deck

Room to rest, and plenty to play with.

  • A shaded sun deck

    Lie in the sun, or sit in the shade when you need a break.

  • A water slide

    Slide straight from the boat into the sea.

  • A floating mat

    Float on the water and rest between swims.

  • There is a toilet on board.
  • You step on board barefoot.
