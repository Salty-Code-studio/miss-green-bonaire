How your cruise day works

From your ship to our boat and back again.

  1. Walk off the ship

    Meet us in the parking lot next to Capriccio Restaurant. It is about a 3 minute walk from the pier.

  2. Ride the bus with our crew

    We take the bus together to the boat.

  3. Four hours on the water

    Snorkel, slide into the sea, float and have a drink. See the list below.

  4. Back to your ship

    We pick a time that fits your ship, so you are back on time.

You're always back on time

  • Ship arrives late? We adjust the tour.
  • Ship can't stop in Bonaire? You get all your money back.

On the boat

  • A welcome drink
  • A drift snorkel
  • A guided snorkel for beginners
  • Water slide and float
  • Open bar

Cruise guests don't pay the Bonaire nature fee (STINAPA).

What to bring

We bring almost everything

  • The bus to the boat
  • Captain and crew
  • A snorkel guide
  • Mask and fins
  • Water slide
  • Floating mat
  • A welcome drink
  • Open bar

You only bring

  • Swimwear
  • Sunscreen
  • A towel

Need a prescription mask? Bring your own.

There are no showers on board. Shoes come off on the boat.

Tell us your ship and date

The price is not online. Ask us and we send it to you, with a time that fits your ship.
